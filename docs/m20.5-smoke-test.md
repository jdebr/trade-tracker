# Ticker Details Panel Smoke Test (M20.5)

A manual pass over the ticker details panel on the live app. Each check lists what to do, what **✅ Pass** looks like, and what counts as **❌ Fail**. Note any ❌ with its number.

**Before you start:** hard-refresh the app so you have the latest frontend.

---

## Part A — Opening and closing

### A1. Every page opens it
**Steps:** Click a ticker symbol on the **Watchlist**, **Screener**, **Positions** (open card and closed table) and **Alerts** pages.
**✅ Pass:** Symbols have a dotted underline; hovering shows "\<company\> · details"; clicking slides a panel in from the right for that ticker.
**❌ Fail:** A symbol isn't clickable, or the panel shows a different ticker.

### A2. Closing
**Steps:** Close with **Esc**, with **✕**, and by clicking the dimmed page.
**✅ Pass:** All three close it, and the page behind is exactly as you left it (sort, filters, scroll).
**❌ Fail:** It won't close, or the page resets.

### A3. Speed
**Steps:** Open a few different tickers in a row.
**✅ Pass:** Content appears in well under a second (a brief skeleton is fine). Reopening the same ticker within a minute is instant.
**❌ Fail:** Multi-second waits, or an error.

### A4. Phone width
**Steps:** Narrow the window to phone width and open a ticker.
**✅ Pass:** The panel fills the screen, scrolls, and nothing overflows sideways.
**❌ Fail:** Horizontal scrolling or clipped content.

---

## Part B — Content

### B1. Header and price
**✅ Pass:** Name and sector · industry under the symbol; the close, a green/red change with %, and "close \<day\>" matching the latest data.
**❌ Fail:** Missing name for a universe ticker, or a change that's clearly wrong vs. the Charts page.

### B2. Recent candles
**Steps:** Open a ticker whose Watchlist Patterns column shows a chip.
**✅ Pass:** The mini chart shows ~3 months of candles with ▲/▼/● markers on some recent bars. The list below groups patterns by day (newest first), each with a one-line meaning, and includes the chip's pattern on the latest day. A ticker with no patterns says so.
**❌ Fail:** No chart, markers on the wrong bars (compare with the list's dates), or the chip's pattern missing from the list.

### B3. Signals
**✅ Pass:** The score matches the Watchlist's Score badge for the same ticker; each active signal shows ✓ or ✗ with its expression.
**❌ Fail:** A different score than the Watchlist, or signals you've disabled appearing.

### B4. Indicators
**Steps:** Hover **RSI (14)**.
**✅ Pass:** Values match the Watchlist row; "as of" shows the data date; the tooltip explains how to read RSI. **vs EMA 50** is green above, red below.
**❌ Fail:** Mismatched values.

### B5. Open position
**Steps:** Open a ticker you hold (simulated is fine).
**✅ Pass:** An **Open position** section with entry, shares, stop, target, and the current R (green if in profit). **View in Positions** goes there and closes the panel. Tickers you don't hold have no such section.
**❌ Fail:** Wrong numbers vs. the Positions page.

---

## Part C — Actions

### C1. Full chart
**Steps:** From a **Screener** ticker that is *not* on your watchlist, click **Full chart**.
**✅ Pass:** The Charts page opens on that ticker (the URL ends `?symbol=XYZ`). Picking another symbol there updates the URL; reloading keeps it.
**❌ Fail:** It opens on your first watchlist ticker instead.

### C2. Add to watchlist
**Steps:** From a Screener ticker not on your watchlist, click **Add to watchlist**.
**✅ Pass:** A brief spinner, then an **In watchlist** tag; the ticker appears on the Watchlist page.
**❌ Fail:** An error, or the button stays after adding.
